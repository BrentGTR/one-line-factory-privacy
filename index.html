Privacy Policy · One Line Factory

Last updated: 17 November 2026
Applies to: One Line Factory (games.onelinefactory), Android

What the game stores on your device

One Line Factory keeps a small settings file inside the app's own storage:

  • which levels you have completed, and the stars you earned
  • your heart balance and when the next heart is due
  • three preferences: sound, haptics, and whether you have seen the tutorial hint

That file never leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud save. Uninstalling the app deletes it. You can also clear it from the in-game Settings screen.

What the advertising service collects

The game shows adverts through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those adverts, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier), or a hashed device identifier
  • your IP address, coarse location derived from it, and device and app information
  • which adverts were shown, and whether they were interacted with

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. Google's own privacy policy applies to it: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google's partner-sites policy explains how advertising data is used: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

  • Personalised advertising. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before requesting personalised adverts, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer at any time from the in-game Settings screen where that option is offered, or by asking the app to show the consent form again.
  • Reset your advertising ID. Android Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. You can delete or reset the advertising ID there. The game keeps working with adverts limited or absent.
  • Decline ads entirely. The game is playable without any advert. A missing advert never blocks progress: no level, retry or heart refill depends on one.
  • Delete your data. Clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Nothing else is held by us.

What the game does not do

  • No account, email address, name or phone number is requested.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service is used.
  • No data is sold.
  • No access to contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • No advertising is shown inside a level, and none is shown after a failed attempt.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It contains no chat, no user-generated content and no social features. If you believe a child has used the app and you want the advertising identifier reset, use the Android settings described above.
